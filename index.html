Phone / tablet: ◀ ▶ bottom-left to steer (or tilt the phone, in Settings). GAS and BRAKE bottom-right. In the air, hold GAS to drop the nose or BRAKE to lift it. Top-right buttons: respawn, camera, pause.
Time Trial: ride any of the 28 tracks alone. Your best lap is replayed as a blue ghost bike, and the number under TIME TRIAL shows if you are ahead (green) or behind (red) at that point on the lap. The top 10 times on every track go on the Leaderboards.
Real Circuits: the name of the next corner appears at the top of the screen as you approach it.
Hitting barriers at a steep angle, animals, people or traffic will throw you off. Land jumps with the bike matching the slope. Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next round.
Real-circuit outlines: Silverstone and Brands Hatch from the TUM racetrack database (OpenStreetMap data, LGPL-3.0); Mugello from bacinger/f1-circuits (MIT). Other real circuits are traced approximations; the TT, North West 200 and Ulster GP courses are scaled down.
